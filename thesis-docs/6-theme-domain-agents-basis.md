# Basis for the Six Domain Theme Agents

## Overview

The six domain theme agents—Infrastructure, Health, Safety, Tourism, Economy, and Environment—were selected to align with the six mandated sectoral chapters that Philippine LGUs must include in their Comprehensive Development Plan under the Local Government Code of 1991 (RA 7160) and the DILG CDP Preparation Guidelines (MC No. 2021-144).

---

## Legal and Institutional Basis

### 1. RA 7160 — Local Government Code of 1991

- **Full citation:** Congress of the Philippines. (1991). *Republic Act No. 7160: The Local Government Code of 1991*. Enacted October 10, 1991.
- **URL:** https://laws.chanrobles.com/republicacts
- **DILG official PDF:** https://www.dilg.gov.ph/reports-and-resources/local-government-code-of-1991/17
- **Relevant sections:**
  - Book III, Title I, Chapter 5 (Sections 113–117): Health, Safety and Sanitation
  - Book III, Title I, Section 16: LGU powers over infrastructure, economic development, tourism, and environmental protection
- **Confidence:** HIGH — official government repository

---

### 2. DILG MC No. 2021-144 — Revised CDP and CLUP Preparation Guidelines

- **Full citation:** Department of the Interior and Local Government. (2021). *MC No. 2021-144: Revised Guidelines on the Preparation and Integration of the Comprehensive Development Plan (CDP) and the Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP) in the Local Development Agenda*. DILG, Quezon City, Philippines.
- **URL:** https://www.dilg.gov.ph/issuances-archive/mc/
- **Relevant content:** Mandates that every LGU's CDP must include sectoral chapters covering: (1) Socio-Political and Social Development (Health), (2) Economic Development (Livelihood/LED), (3) Physical/Infrastructure Development (Transportation, Utilities), (4) Environmental Management, (5) Disaster Risk Reduction and Management, (6) Tourism and Cultural Heritage.
- **Confidence:** HIGH — official DILG issuances archive

## Academic and Institutional Sources

### 7. Abacahan, G.M.M. et al. (2026) — Walking-Centric Urban Livability Assessment of Baguio City

- **Full citation:** Abacahan, G. M. M., Chua, A. R. T., Monzon, M. F. B., Samson, A. A. V., & Claridades, A. R. C. (2026). A walking-centric urban livability assessment of Baguio City using open-source GIS data. *ISPRS Annals of Photogrammetry, Remote Sensing and Spatial Information Sciences*, X-5/W4-2025, 3–10. https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-annals-X-5-W4-2025-3-2026
- **URL:** https://isprs-annals.copernicus.org/articles/X-5-W4-2025/3/2026/
- **Open access:** Yes — CC-BY-4.0
- **Validates:** Domains 1 (Infrastructure/convenience), 2 (Health), 3 (Safety), 6 (Environment)
- **Key evidence:** Confirms Baguio City government operates an active institutional livability assessment system based on WHO's 4 dimensions (convenience, amenity, health, safety), directly mapping to 4 of the 6 theme domains.
